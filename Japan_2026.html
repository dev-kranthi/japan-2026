🏔 Phase 2 — Hakone · Days 5–6 · 31 May–2 Jun 2026 · 2 nights ryokan · Car from Shinjuku
Day 5 · 31 May 2026 (Sun)
Car Pickup Shinjuku · Kawaguchiko (Fuji) · Hakone · Ropeway · Lake Ashi · Hakone Skyline · Onsen · Kaiseki
⛅ 21°C · Mt Fuji: 40% chance of clear views
Activities
08:00
Pick up Toyota Rent a Car, Shinjuku branch. All bags already in car from hotel. Request English GPS + ETC card at pickup. Drive west via Tomei Expressway. Tolls ~¥1,500 to Kawaguchiko.
09:30
Lake Kawaguchiko — Fuji views. All. Park at lakeside (free). Fuji reflected in the lake on clear mornings. One of the best unobstructed Fuji viewpoints in Japan. 45 min stop. Shilpa: best landscape photo of the trip.
11:00
Drive Irohazaka — 28-hairpin switchback road into Hakone. Dramatic mountain ascent. Use low gears on descent. Arrive ryokan ~11:30. Drop bags and change into yukata.
12:00
Hakone Ropeway over Owakudani. All. Active volcanic crater, sulfur vents, eerie steam. Buy kuro-tamago (black eggs boiled in volcanic springs — each adds 7 years to your life). Drive to ropeway — free parking.
14:00
Lake Ashi pirate ship cruise. All. Fuji reflected in the water on still days. Park at Moto-Hakone pier — free parking. 30 min cruise.
15:30
Hakone Skyline drive. All (in car). 5km ridge road, 58 bends, panoramic views of Fuji and Lake Ashi simultaneously. Drive at your own pace. Toll ¥360. Famed among driving enthusiasts — this road does not exist on the Free Pass circuit.
16:30
Hakone Open Air Museum. All. Henry Moore bronzes, Picasso pavilion, glass prism tower Ariv can climb inside. Free parking.
18:00
Private family onsen (kashikiri). Book 18:00 slot at check-in. Also book 06:00 slot for tomorrow morning.
19:00
Kaiseki dinner at ryokan. Shilpa & Ariv: vegetarian kaiseki (pre-confirmed — no meat, no fish). Kranthi: standard kaiseki — no pork or beef (pre-confirmed). 7–10 courses placed individually.

Dietary Phrases — Show to Restaurant Staff
Tap any Japanese text to copy it to your clipboard, then paste into Google Translate or show your phone to the staff.
Shilpa & Ariv — Core phrase (show this first)
肉と魚介類は食べられません。卵と乳製品は大丈夫です。
"We cannot eat meat or seafood. Eggs and dairy are fine."
Kranthi — Core phrase
豚肉と牛肉は食べられません。
"I cannot eat pork or beef."
No fish stock (dashi) — very important in Japan
だしは使わないでください。
"Please do not use fish stock/dashi."
Do you have a vegetarian menu?
ベジタリアンメニューはありますか？
"Do you have a vegetarian menu?"
Is this vegetables only?
これは野菜だけですか？
"Is this vegetables only?"
No fish flakes (Nikko yuba / miso soups)
かつおぶしは使わないでください。
"Please do not use fish flakes (katsuobushi)."
Vegetable skewers (yakitori)
野菜串焼きをください。
"Vegetable skewers please."
Vegetable okonomiyaki
野菜のお好み焼きをください。肉と魚介類なしで。
"Vegetable okonomiyaki please, no meat or seafood."
General Phrases
Thank you very much
ありがとうございます。
"Arigatou gozaimasu" — thank you very much.
Excuse me / sorry
すみません。
"Sumimasen" — excuse me / to get attention.
How much is this?
これはいくらですか？
"Kore wa ikura desu ka?" — How much is this?
Where is the toilet?
トイレはどこですか？
"Toire wa doko desu ka?"
